          Real visits = visits Google counts as engaged (stayed 10+ seconds, viewed 2+ pages, or contacted you) and where the page was actually on screen. Google's own "engaged" figure is shown alongside, because it also counts tabs left open in the background and bots that hold a page open. Visits that leave instantly are mostly bots and crawlers, so they're in the total but never counted as real.

          How "on screen" is worked out: visits are grouped by day, source, town, device, browser and screen size. Groups with zero seconds on screen don't count as real; a group with on-screen time but no "engaged" flag (a page left open and returned to) counts as one real visit.

          Messages counts only contact-form sends that Google Analytics records. Opening the contact page is shown separately and is not a message.

          Visits from the office connection are excluded in Google Analytics, so the team browsing the sites doesn't count. Phones on mobile data or a VPN can still show up.

          Everything comes live from the Google Analytics 4 Data API and the Search Console API. Use the "Check in Google Analytics" link on any site to compare these numbers against Google's own screens. No IP addresses are collected or shown, because Google doesn't expose them.
