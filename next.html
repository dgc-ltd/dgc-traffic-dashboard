How to read this page (tap to open or close)
VisitOne time someone opens one of our websites. If the same person comes back tomorrow, that's 2 visits.
Real personA visit where someone actually had our page on their screen and looked at it: for 10 seconds or more, or looked at 2+ pages, or sent us a message.
RobotsComputer programs that open websites automatically (search engines checking pages, spam tools). They make the numbers look bigger, so we don't count them as real people.
Typed our address / saved linkThey typed the address in, used a bookmark, or tapped a link in WhatsApp, a text, an email or a QR code. People we give our address to (cards, vans, quotes) show up here.
Google search (free)They searched on Google and clicked our normal listing. We don't pay for these. Getting more of them is what SEO is about.
Google Ads (paid)They clicked our advert on Google. We pay Google for every one of these clicks.
First pageThe first page someone sees when they arrive: often the page Google or an advert sent them to.
Time readingHow long our page was actually on their screen. A tab left open in the background doesn't count.
SEOShort for "search engine optimisation": making the website easy for Google to understand, so Google shows it higher up, for free, when people search.
Position in GoogleWhere we appear in Google's list. 1 is the very top. 10 is the bottom of the first page. 11 or more is page two, which hardly anyone looks at.

            Visits from the office internet connection are left out, so the team looking at our own websites doesn't count. Phones on mobile data or a VPN can still show up.

            All numbers come live from Google Analytics and Google Search Console. The "Check in Google Analytics" link on each site opens Google's own screens so you can compare. No IP addresses are collected or shown.

            How "real people" is worked out: visits are grouped by day, source, town, device, browser and screen size. A group with zero seconds on screen has no real people, even if Google marked it "engaged". A group with time on screen but no "engaged" flag (a page left open and returned to) counts as one real person.
